## 🪞 Project Reflection
## 📘 What I Learned
Through working on this project, I gained practical experience in data analysis and dashboard creation. I learned how to clean and prepare raw data by handling missing values, correcting data types, and ensuring data consistency before analysis. This helped me understand the importance of data quality in producing accurate and reliable results.
I also developed my data visualization skills by selecting appropriate charts and designing dashboards that clearly present insights. The project improved my ability to focus on key metrics and KPIs, and taught me how to present data in a clear, simple, and user-friendly way. Additionally, I learned how to analyze data trends and extract meaningful insights that support decision-making.
## ⚠️ What Was Challenging
One of the main challenges was choosing the most suitable visualizations for the data and organizing the dashboard layout effectively. At the beginning, it was difficult to decide which charts best represented the insights and how to arrange them in a logical flow.
Another challenge was dealing with data inconsistencies during the data preparation stage. Fixing errors and ensuring the data was clean required attention to detail and patience. However, these challenges helped me improve my problem-solving skills and gain more confidence in working with data analysis tools.
