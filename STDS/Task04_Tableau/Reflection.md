## 📝 Project Reflection
## ✅ What I Learned
How to analyze pharmacy sales data and identify key business insights.
Building interactive dashboards in Tableau using different types of visualizations.
Choosing the right chart for each analysis (sales trends, product performance, customer behavior).
Using filters and dashboard layout to make insights clear and easy to understand.
Thinking from a business perspective, not just visualizing data.
## ⚠️ What Was Challenging
Deciding which metrics are the most important and should appear on the dashboard.
Designing a clean and organized dashboard layout without overcrowding visuals.
Connecting product and customer insights together in a meaningful way.
Making sure the dashboards tell a clear story and not just show numbers.
## 🚀 What I Improved from Previous Tasks
Compared to my previous projects using Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, this project helped me:
Better understand how to structure a full analytics project from data to dashboards.
Improve my skills in Tableau dashboard design and interactivity.
Become more confident in comparing insights across different tools.
Develop stronger analytical thinking when answering business questions using data.
